How to Limit One Problem per AI Chat

How to keep one problem per session so AI answers in Clincoo stay focused and accurate.

Multiple problems at once make AI lose context and mix answers.

One problem, one chat

In editor.clincoo.buzz open a new chat for each bug. Mention only one symptom and one related file.

Summarize before continuing

On app.clincoo.buzz after the answer, ask for a summary of the solution before moving to the next problem. Record on blog.clincoo.buzz.
