How to Check AI Results Before Applying in Clincoo

How to verify AI suggestions in preview and console before committing changes.

AI suggestions can miss context. Always test in the Clincoo environment before applying.

Test in preview and console

In editor.clincoo.buzz apply the change on a test branch or copy. Open the console on app.clincoo.buzz and confirm no new errors.

Compare before and after

Record behavior before and after on blog.clincoo.buzz. If there is a regression, ask AI to revise with evidence.
