How to Ask AI for Reproduction Steps When Stuck

How to request clear reproduction steps from AI so a Clincoo problem can be repeated and fixed.

Without reproduction steps, AI often guesses the cause. Ask for steps others can follow.

Give context and request steps

In editor.clincoo.buzz explain expected vs actual behavior and ask AI to write 1-2-3 steps to repeat the error. Include the full error message.

Test the steps yourself

On app.clincoo.buzz follow the steps AI gave. If they cannot be repeated, ask for a revision. Record on blog.clincoo.buzz.
