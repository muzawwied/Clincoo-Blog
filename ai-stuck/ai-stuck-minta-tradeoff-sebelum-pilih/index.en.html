How to Ask AI to Explain Trade-offs Before Choosing a Solution

How to request AI explain pros and cons of each solution before choosing in Clincoo.

AI often gives one solution immediately. Ask for trade-offs so you can pick what fits the Clincoo context.

Request a list of pros and cons

In editor.clincoo.buzz after AI proposes a solution, ask 'explain the trade-offs of 2-3 approaches: performance, maintainability, and browser compatibility'.

Choose based on project constraints

On app.clincoo.buzz compare with target browsers and bundle size. Record the decision on blog.clincoo.buzz.
