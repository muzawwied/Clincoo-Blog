How to Verify an AI Solution in Two Browsers

How to test an AI suggestion in Chrome and Firefox before applying it in Clincoo.

A solution that works in one browser can fail in another. Test at least two.

Test in Chrome and Firefox

In editor.clincoo.buzz apply the change, then open the preview in Chrome and Firefox. Note console differences.

Fix before commit

If they differ, ask AI to adjust for both engines. Check on app.clincoo.buzz and save the note on blog.clincoo.buzz.
