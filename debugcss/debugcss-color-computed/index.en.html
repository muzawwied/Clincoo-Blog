How to See the Winning Color in Computed

How to use the Computed tab to find the final color value in Clincoo.

The displayed color often differs from what was written because of the cascade. Computed shows the final result.

Open Computed color

In editor.clincoo.buzz select the element, open Computed, and look for color or background-color. Click to see the source.

Compare with design

On app.clincoo.buzz confirm contrast is sufficient. Note the final value on blog.clincoo.buzz.
