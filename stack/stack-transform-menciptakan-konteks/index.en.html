How Transform Creates a New Stacking Context

How transform, opacity, or filter creates a new stacking context in Clincoo so a child's z-index cannot escape.

An element with transform: translateZ(0) or opacity less than 1 creates its own stacking context. Children inside cannot z-index above siblings outside.

Check in DevTools

In editor.clincoo.buzz open the Layers panel. If a dropdown is covered, look for an ancestor with transform or filter. Remove it temporarily to test.

Common fix

On app.clincoo.buzz move the element that must be on top outside the context, or raise z-index on the parent context. Record it on blog.clincoo.buzz.
