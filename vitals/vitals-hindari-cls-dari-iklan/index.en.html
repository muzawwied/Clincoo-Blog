How to Avoid CLS from Ad Slots or Embeds

How to reserve space for ads so they do not cause layout shift in Clincoo.

Ads or embeds that appear late often push content and trigger high CLS.

Reserve slot height

In editor.clincoo.buzz give min-height or aspect-ratio to the ad container before it loads. Use a placeholder of the same size.

Measure after deploy

In app.clincoo.buzz run Lighthouse and ensure CLS is under 0.1. Document it on blog.clincoo.buzz.
